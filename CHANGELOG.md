# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories:

**Added** (new features),
**Fixed** (bug and behaviour fixes),
**Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## 2026-10-29

### Added
- **Downloads**: ready-to-run versions for Linux and Windows.

## 2026-10-28

### Added
- **Berlin start**: maps now start in Berlin, and switching between Google and Apple
  Maps keeps you at the same place.
- **Rating progress**: the host sees who has finished rating.
- **"Doesn't fit" rating**: mark a find that does not match its category.

### Fixed
- **Fairer scores**: one strict or generous player no longer skews the results.
- **Empty categories**: they no longer show an old picture during review.

### Info
- **Review pace**: the review moves on as soon as everyone is ready.

## 2026-10-21

### Added
- **Live map links**: share your find as a live map link instead of a screenshot.
- **Review and results**: rate each other's finds with stars and see the final results.
- **Chat and timer**: chat and a countdown while you explore.

### Fixed
- **Player impersonation**: players can no longer pose as someone else.
- **Google consent**: the Google Maps consent screen no longer gets in the way.

## 2026-10-20

### Added
- **First release**: a multiplayer party game where friends race to find places on
  Google or Apple Maps, then rate each other's finds.
